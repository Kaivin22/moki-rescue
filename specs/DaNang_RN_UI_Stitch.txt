a smart travel itinerary planner for Da Nang, Vietnam.

Platform : React Native (iOS + Android)
Language : Vietnamese (vi-VN)

Aesthetic: "Bold Nature" — premium outdoor travel app.
  Think: high-contrast nature photography book meets
         modern travel startup app.
  NOT: pastel soft, NOT: corporate blue, NOT: childish.

Key visual signatures:
  - Dark forest green headers create premium, grounded feel
  - Lime accent pops sharply on dark backgrounds
  - Cards: white border-radius 16px on mint (#E4E9D5) background
  - Buttons: full-width, 52px height, border-radius 26px (pill shape)
    CTA = lime fill with dark green text
    Secondary = dark green outline with dark green text
  - Typography: bold large numbers, clear hierarchy
  - Icons: outline style, sage color on white bg;
           lime color on dark green bg
  - Bottom tab: dark green background with lime active indicator
  - Status bar: dark green background, white icons
